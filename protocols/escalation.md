# Escalation Protocol

Escalation returns a consequential or blocked choice to the role that owns its authority. It is not a substitute for ordinary coordination.

## Escalate when

- the mission, intended behavior, or acceptance meaning is materially ambiguous;
- two roles claim the same mutable boundary;
- the requested action involves publication, money, credentials, permissions, privacy, destructive changes, or merge authority without explicit authorization;
- a stable interface or approved decision must change;
- evidence cannot support the requested completion claim;
- the same approach has failed twice and the missing fact or new direction is not available;
- a role cannot complete a safe handoff.

## Escalation record

State:

1. the blocked outcome;
2. observed evidence and attempted approaches;
3. the exact missing decision or authority;
4. available options and meaningful trade-offs;
5. the owner who can decide;
6. work that can safely continue while blocked.

Create a Decision record when the answer changes project truth. Update affected work items after the decision; do not keep executing from stale authority.

## Do not escalate

Do not ask the user to perform routine branch, commit, status, or routing mechanics already owned by the team. Do not create approval gates for local reversible choices unless a concrete project risk requires them.
